Calculation Settings

Safety stock har item ke liye ADS ke hisab se banta hai: Safety Stock = ADS × safety days. Cover days aur analysis window Reorder Suggestions tab se badalte hain.

Discontinued items suggestions mein nahi aate. Supplier mapping kabhi guess nahi hoti — jis item ka supplier POS mein mapped nahi, woh “Supplier not mapped” likh kar alag nazar aata hai taake pehle mapping theek ki jaye.

Backup & Restore

Assistant ki apni local data (settings, supplier mappings, overrides, audit trail) ki backup file banti hai — POS ka data is mein shaamil nahi, woh hamesha POS_DB se fresh fetch hota hai.

Backup mein shaamil: connection settings, safety-stock setting, manual overrides (reasons ke saath), audit trail.
